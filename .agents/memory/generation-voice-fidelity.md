Generated examples must follow the project's target voice, constraints, and golden exemplar, never the provider's default alignment style. In the user's words, the reason they built the app is to avoid examples that pathologize the user, tell them how to interpret reality, or assume perceptive and logical superiority.

**Why:** the user said these defaults defeat the purpose of the app.

**How to apply:** Any generation, regeneration, or conversion prompt must keep the project-fidelity rules: no disclaimers, moralizing, pathologizing, reframing, or condescension unless the project asks for it.
